Experience

Telenet × BASE
boot camp

September 2025 · Labenne, France

In September 2025, I took part in a weeklong boot camp in Labenne, France, through Thomas More University of Applied Sciences. The university put us into teams to work on a challenge for Telenet’s BASE app.

Over the week, we came up with different ideas and sketched out how each one could work. Once we picked a direction, we turned our sketches into a Figma prototype so we could show what the idea would look like in the app and how someone would use it.

At the end of the week, we presented our proposal and walked the Telenet team through the prototype. Our team won the challenge, and Telenet is now developing the solution for the BASE app.
